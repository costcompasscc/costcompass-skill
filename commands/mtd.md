---
description: Show month-to-date CostCompass spend, optionally for one service.
argument-hint: "[service]"
allowed-tools: Bash
---

Use the `costcompass:spend` skill to report month-to-date spend.

If `$ARGUMENTS` names a service, report that service; otherwise report the
all-services total. Follow the skill's command, JSON-field, and presentation
rules exactly — do not restate or reinterpret them here.
